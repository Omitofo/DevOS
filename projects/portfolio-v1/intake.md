## 1. Project Identity

- **Working Title:** Personal Portfolio v1
- **One-sentence pitch:** A clean, elegant, fast personal website that showcases my work and makes it easy for potential clients to contact me.
- **Primary owner / decision maker:** Renatus Cartesius

## 2. Problem Statement

I currently share my work through scattered links (GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Drive).  
Potential clients often say it is hard to get a quick overview of what I do and how to hire me.  
I need a single, professional place that presents my selected projects and makes contacting me frictionless.

## 3. Success Definition

- A visitor can understand what I do and see 4–6 selected projects within 60 seconds.
- Contact form or clear email/CTA is available on every page.
- The site loads quickly on mobile and desktop.
- I can update project content myself without touching code (or with very little friction).
- The site feels calm, modern, and trustworthy — not too flashy nor template-looking.

## 4. Constraints

- Timeline / hard deadlines: First usable version within 2–3 days of starting implementation.
- Budget / team size: Solo (me + AI assistance). No paid team.
- Must-use technologies or platforms: Prefer modern web technologies. I am comfortable with React/Next.js or similar. Hosting should be simple (Vercel, Netlify, or equivalent).
- Must-avoid technologies or approaches: Heavy animation libraries, complex CMS, or anything that requires a backend server I have to maintain.
- Regulatory / compliance requirements: Basic privacy (contact form data). No special certifications needed.
- Existing systems that must integrate: None. This is a greenfield site.

## 5. Audience & Context

- Primary users: Potential freelance clients (startups, small agencies, product teams) looking for a designer/developer.
- Secondary users: Peers, future employers, or collaborators who want a quick sense of my work.
- Business / organizational context: Personal brand / freelance practice.
- Geographic or cultural considerations: Primarily English-speaking audience. Design should feel international and clean.

## 6. Scope Boundaries

**In scope:**  
- Home / landing view with short introduction and selected work
- Project detail pages (or case-study style sections)
- About section
- Contact method (form or clear email + links)
- Responsive design (mobile + desktop)
- Basic SEO (title, meta description, clean URLs)

**Explicitly out of scope:**  
- Blog or articles
- Client login / dashboard
- E-commerce or payments
- Multi-language support (for v1)
- Dark/light mode toggle (nice-to-have later, not required now)
- Complex animations or interactive 3D elements
